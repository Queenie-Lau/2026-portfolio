About me :)

Hello! I’m Queenie, a cinematic photographer from LA ready (and ecstatic woo!) to bring your creative vision to life!! :)

I’ve fallen in love with photography as a way of turning feelings, memories, and everyday moments into something cinematic. My mom was one of my first subjects. I started by helping her take photos for her yoga practice, which taught me how to notice light, movement, shape, and the quiet confidence that comes through when someone feels comfortable being seen.

I first learned how to tell stories through video editing (mostly lyrics videos and multifandoms!) and that still shapes the way I see. I think a lot about styling, color, framing, and the story I'm trying to tell when I photograph. But most importantly, I try and adapt to you when photographing so I can capture your most authentic self!

I’m drawn to creating images that feel alive, expressive, and cinematic, the kind that can hold a feeling and shift between dreamy portraiture, editorial storytelling, and fashion-inspired worlds. I love building visual worlds with people, whether it begins with a concept, a mood, a song, a location, or just a feeling.

My work is rooted in visual storytelling, play, and emotion. I’m especially interested in images that feel like visual poems or essays, something colorful andintentional, a little dreamlike, and personal to the people in them.

From concept to final image

A thoughtful, collaborative process

01

Creative Direction

We can shape the concept together through moodboards, references, color palettes, styling ideas, locations, and the emotional story of the shoot

02

On Set

I’ll guide you gently through posing and movement while leaving room for natural, playful, organic moments!

03

Color & Delivery

I'll cull the photos after our shoot and curate a first-pass selection with test color grades for you to review and choose from. With your feedback, I’ll finalize the color and deliver the images in high-resolution digital format :)
